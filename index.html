The AI workflow you'll practise: input → process → insight → action

The repeatable pattern from Module 3, applied to a typical weekly sales review.

InputExport sales, ARPU, churn and conversion data from your systems.
ProcessAI cleans the data and spots trends, gaps and outliers.
InsightA dashboard and one-page summary show what changed and why.
ActionAI drafts the team update, next-week focus and customer follow-ups.
AutomateAn assistant repeats the routine every week — you review and send.
AI drafts — people decide. Participants learn to check outputs for accuracy, protect customer data and follow company policy before anything reaches a customer or manager.
